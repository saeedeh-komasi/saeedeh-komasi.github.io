MASTER'S THESIS

Effect of Heterogeneity on Salt Dispersion During Low-Salinity Water Injection with Polymer

A numerical investigation of the influence of reservoir heterogeneity on salt transport, polymer behavior, and oil recovery during low-salinity water injection.

Degree M.Sc. Petroleum Engineering
University Iran University of Science and Technology
Period 2019 — 2022

RESEARCH QUESTION

How does reservoir heterogeneity influence salt dispersion during low-salinity water injection?

Low-salinity water injection can influence oil recovery through several physicochemical mechanisms. However, reservoir heterogeneity can significantly affect the transport and distribution of injected water and dissolved species.

This research investigated how different spatial distributions of porosity and permeability influence salt dispersion and oil recovery during low-salinity water injection combined with polymer flooding.

METHODOLOGY

From reservoir heterogeneity to numerical simulation.

03

Evaluated low-salinity water injection combined with polymer under different reservoir conditions.

04

Investigated oil recovery, salt dispersion, and polymer effects for different reservoir and fluid conditions.
