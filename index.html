Synthesis and evaluation of a salinity-resistant natural surfactant

For enhanced oil recovery from a tight carbonate reservoir, I designed and ran a complete set of spontaneous-imbibition experiments comparing a natural surfactant against low-salinity water and conventional surfactants.

Core samples, crude oil, and test solutions were prepared and evaluated end-to-end, then interpreted to identify which mechanisms were actually driving the improvement in recovery — not just that recovery improved.
