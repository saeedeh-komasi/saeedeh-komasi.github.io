RESEARCH & PROJECTS

Exploring reservoir
behavior through research.

My research interests lie at the intersection of reservoir engineering, geochemistry, special core analysis, and enhanced oil recovery. I combine laboratory experiments, numerical simulation, and data analysis to investigate reservoir behavior and identify the mechanisms behind observed results.

01

RESERVOIR GEOCHEMISTRY

Injection Water Selection & Scale Risk Assessment

Overview

Conducted reservoir studies to identify suitable injection-water scenarios while minimizing the risk of mineral scale formation in an Iranian oil field.

Formation water and seawater were evaluated under different reservoir conditions, including their interaction with reservoir rock.

Approach

  • Formation water and seawater compatibility
  • Water–rock interaction studies
  • Geochemical simulation
  • Carbonate and sulfate scale prediction
  • Laboratory validation
  • Risk assessment
PHREEQC OLI Geochemical Simulation Scale Prediction Laboratory Validation
02

ENHANCED OIL RECOVERY

Natural Surfactant for Enhanced Oil Recovery

Research Question

Can a salinity-resistant natural surfactant improve oil recovery from a tight carbonate reservoir compared with conventional reference conditions?

Research

A natural surfactant was extracted and evaluated through spontaneous imbibition experiments. Its performance was compared with low-salinity water and conventional surfactants.

~30%
increase in oil recovery factor

compared with the reference condition.

03

SPECIAL CORE ANALYSIS

SCAL Data Analysis & Laboratory QC

Overview

Analyzed relative permeability and capillary pressure data obtained from centrifuge experiments and evaluated the quality and physical consistency of laboratory datasets.

The analysis focused on identifying unrealistic curves, abnormal saturation values, and noisy measurements.

Analysis

  • Relative permeability interpretation
  • Capillary pressure analysis
  • Saturation QC
  • Curve consistency assessment
  • Data noise identification
  • Technical reporting

MASTER'S THESIS

Effect of Heterogeneity on Salt Dispersion During Low-Salinity Water Injection with Polymer

Research Focus

Investigated the effect of reservoir heterogeneity on salt dispersion during low-salinity water injection combined with polymer flooding.

Numerical Study

Designed and simulated 91 heterogeneous reservoir models with different porosity and permeability distributions using two-dimensional and two-phase reservoir models.

Key Variables
